## What Are Lensa Magic Avatars?

**Lensa Magic Avatars are AI-generated portraits that turn your selfies into artwork in different styles, like fantasy, anime, cosmic or cartoon.**

You upload a set of selfies, and Lensa's AI learns what your face looks like. It then creates dozens of new portraits that place you into different art styles and scenes. Each avatar is unique, so no two packs look exactly the same.

Magic Avatars are made by **Prisma Labs**, the company behind Lensa. The feature is built on AI image generation technology, specifically a version of Stable Diffusion personalized with your photos.

## How to Use Lensa AI Magic Avatars: Step by Step

**Download Lensa, open Magic Avatars, choose your subject and styles, upload 10 to 20 selfies, pay for a pack and wait for your avatars.**

### Step 1: Download and Open Lensa AI

Install **Lensa AI: Photo Editor** from the Apple App Store or Google Play. Open the app and sign in or create an account if asked.

Prisma Labs also offers a separate app called **Avatar Maker & AI Art** on the App Store, which focuses on avatars. This guide uses the main Lensa app.

### Step 2: Tap Magic Avatars

On the main screen, look for the **Magic Avatars** banner or button. Tap it to start a new avatar pack. If you've made avatars before, you'll also see your previous packs here.

### Step 3: Choose Your Subject

Select whether you're creating avatars of a **person** or an **animal**, such as a pet. For people, you'll usually be asked to choose a gender option, which helps the AI pick suitable styles.

### Step 4: Pick Your Styles

According to Elegant Themes, Lensa offers **30+ styles**, and you can select **up to ten**. Popular styles include fantasy, anime, cosmic, fairy, superhero, pop art and cartoon.

**Tip:** More styles mean more variety, but each style gets fewer images and generation takes longer. If you want lots of options in one look, choose fewer styles.

### Step 5: Upload 10 to 20 Selfies

Tap the upload button and select **10 to 20 photos** from your gallery. Use the menu in the top corner to browse other folders if needed.

Lensa works best with:

- **Close-up selfies**
- **Photos of the same person only**
- **Adults**
- **Different angles, expressions and backgrounds**

The quality of these photos is the single biggest factor in how good your avatars look, so take your time here. See the photo tips section below.

### Step 6: Buy a Pack or Use Your Subscription

Choose an avatar pack and pay through your App Store or Google Play account. The price depends on the number of avatars and styles. Subscribers may skip this screen or get a discount, depending on their plan.

### Step 7: Wait for Your Avatars

Lensa now trains the AI on your face and generates your avatars. Elegant Themes estimates about **20 minutes**, though it can take longer during busy periods or with more styles. You can turn on notifications and keep using your phone.

### Step 8: Review, Save and Share

When your pack is ready, you'll get a notification. Open it to browse your avatars by style.

- **Tap an avatar** to view it full screen
- **Save** your favorites to your camera roll
- **Share** directly to Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp or other apps
- **Delete** any you don't like

### Step 9: Edit Your Favorites (Optional)

You can open saved avatars in Lensa's photo editor to adjust lighting, crop for profile pictures or apply effects. Newer versions of Lensa also include AI effects and an advanced eraser for removing unwanted objects.

## Best Photos to Upload for Better Lensa Avatars

**Choose clear, well-lit close-ups of only you, with different angles, expressions and backgrounds, and avoid group shots, filters and heavy makeup changes.**

### Do

- **Use 15 to 20 photos** rather than the minimum 10
- **Show your face clearly**, filling much of the frame
- **Use good, natural lighting**
- **Vary your angles**: front, slight left, slight right
- **Vary expressions**: smiling, neutral, serious
- **Vary backgrounds and outfits**
- **Include a few head-and-shoulder shots**

### Don't

- **Use group photos** or photos with other faces in the frame
- **Use filters**, beauty effects or heavily edited photos
- **Use blurry, dark or low-resolution images**
- **Use photos where your face is covered** by sunglasses, masks or hands
- **Mix very different looks**, like heavy costume makeup or very old photos
- **Upload photos of children**, which Lensa's guidance excludes
- **Upload nude or revealing photos**

### Why Variety Matters

The AI learns your face from patterns across your photos. If every photo has the same angle, lighting and background, the AI may copy those details into every avatar. Variety helps it focus on your actual features.

## How to Get the Best Results From Lensa Magic Avatars

**Use good photos, choose fewer styles for more variety in each, generate a second pack if needed and pick the best avatars rather than expecting every one to be perfect.**

- **Expect some misses:** Not every avatar will look like you. Most people keep their favorites and discard the rest.
- **Focus on styles that suit you:** If fantasy works better than anime, choose more fantasy-style packs next time.
- **Retake photos if results look off:** Poor likeness usually means the photos need improving.
- **Avoid uploading duplicates:** Very similar photos add little information.
- **Use avatars that look natural** for professional profiles, and save the wilder styles for fun.

## Common Lensa Magic Avatar Problems and Fixes

**Most problems come from poor photos, the wrong number of photos or app glitches, and most can be fixed by improving your uploads or restarting the app.**

### Avatars Don't Look Like You

**Upload more varied, clearer close-ups and remove photos with filters, sunglasses or other people.**

### Distorted Faces, Hands or Extra Fingers

**This is a common AI image problem. Discard those avatars and keep the best ones, or generate a new pack.**

### Avatars Look Too Sexualized

**Some users, especially women, have reported overly sexualized results even from ordinary photos. Use only clothed, head-and-shoulder photos, report problematic outputs in the app and delete anything you're uncomfortable with.**

### Skin Tone or Features Changed

**Some users have reported avatars that changed their skin tone or features. Upload photos with natural lighting and no filters, and report biased outputs to Lensa.**

### Upload Fails or Gets Stuck

**Check your internet connection, close and reopen the app, make sure Lensa is updated and try uploading fewer photos at a time.**

### Taking Too Long

**Generation can take longer during busy times. Turn on notifications and check back later rather than closing the app repeatedly.**

### Payment Went Through but No Avatars

**Restart the app, check your previous packs and use "Restore Purchases" in settings. If that doesn't work, contact Lensa support or request a refund through Apple or Google.**

## Privacy: What to Know Before You Upload

**Magic Avatars require you to upload photos of your face, so read Lensa's privacy policy and think about how your images may be used.**

Uploading 10 to 20 face photos to any AI app is a real privacy decision. Some reviewers, including Elegant Themes, have raised concerns about how app privacy policies handle user data.

Before you upload:

- **Read Lensa's current privacy policy and terms**
- **Check how long uploaded photos are kept** and how to request deletion
- **Avoid uploading photos of other people** without their permission
- **Never upload photos of children**
- **Don't include sensitive details**, like documents or addresses, in the background
- **Delete your account and data** if you stop using the app

## Can You Use Lensa Avatars Commercially?

**Check Lensa's terms of service before using avatars for business, and remember that purely AI-generated images generally can't be copyrighted in the US.**

You can usually use avatars for personal profiles and social media. For commercial uses, like logos, ads or merchandise, read Lensa's terms carefully. In the US, courts have confirmed that works created entirely by AI without meaningful human authorship can't be copyrighted, so you may not be able to stop others from copying them.

It's also worth knowing about the debate around AI art. Many artists have objected to AI image tools being trained on their work without permission, and Lensa was at the center of that controversy in 2022.
